### 3. The Transformation Engine: T&M to Outcome-Based
One of the core innovations is the automated conversion of **Time & Materials (T&M)** contracts into **Outcome-Based** contracts.

#### The Logic:
1. **Leakage Detection**: The system scans for "FTE", "Hourly Rate", and "Best Efforts" language.
2. **Ceiling Identification**: It identifies the "Effort-Based" ceiling where the provider is at risk of losing margin if productivity exceeds the cap.
3. **Outcome Mapping**: The AI proposes 3–5 high-value milestones (e.g., "Architecture Sign-off", "MVP Delivery") with specific dollar values.
4. **Compensation Rewrite**: The Drafter agent generates a legal rewrite that links payment to these milestones rather than hours worked, protecting the provider's margin and ensuring client satisfaction through results.
